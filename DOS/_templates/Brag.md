## Conventions

- **Append-only.** Don't groom away past entries. The point is to keep the receipts.
- **Date-stamped, tagged.** Tags let you filter for resume / review purposes:
  - `#shipped` — concrete deliverable, artefact, output, or hands-on technical work
  - `#capability` — new behaviour pattern landed cleanly, with an observable outcome
  - `#recognition` — explicit external feedback / trust signal, with consequences
  - `#relationship` — people / team / peer / manager wins
  - `#strategic` — strategic-altitude work or framing
  - `#process` — process / system built or improved
  - `#scale` — work that landed at increased scope (more people, larger team, broader remit)
  - `#team-outcome` — a win the team delivered that you are accountable-for as manager
- **One-line summary first**, then 1–3 lines of "what made it significant."
- **Roles change.** Add a role line when you change companies / titles, so future-you can
  scope-filter.
- **Team outcomes ARE claimable as management wins — especially for resume purposes.** A
  manager's resume is built on what the team shipped *under their leadership*. Bank
  team-delivered wins here **without over-qualifying them into "I only did the coordination
  part."** Don't route a strong team outcome to Growth just because you didn't type the code.
  - **The resume ↔ review split:** for **resume** bullets, claim the *team outcome*
    (accountable-for framing). For the **review**, keep honest attribution of your *specific*
    contribution vs the team's. Reviews reward what you personally added; resumes reward what
    you were accountable for. The same entry serves both — `#team-outcome` flags which framing
    to use where.
- **Don't grade-inflate.** Landing a routine 1:1 isn't a brag. When a candidate is borderline,
  default to `Growth.md` Reflections — this file's signal degrades the moment the bar drifts.
